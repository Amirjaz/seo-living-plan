# Field lessons

These lessons come from running this method on seven live sites (five medical and dental practices, one local restaurant business, one new online shop) between August and November 2026. Evidence is summarised without client names. Use them to seed a new `lessons.md` — copy the rows that fit the user's sites, keep the IDs, and add the user's own evidence as it arrives. Don't present them as universal rules: confidence and status say how far each one has been tested.

Confidence: `once` → `2+ sites` → `reliable`. Status: `active`, `open` (outcome pending), `disputed` (contrary evidence exists).

| ID | type | lesson | evidence (summarised) | confidence | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FL-01 | all | Search Console "Request indexing" allows only ~5–6 URLs a day per property; a repeated request burns a slot and doesn't move the URL up. Plan ≤ 5 a day, never repeat a URL within 14 days. | 7th request refused "Quota exceeded" on a local-business property; a medical property refused the same after its live test | 2+ sites | active |
| FL-02 | all | Search Console lags ~2 days; end windows on the latest complete day or the newest week looks like a drop. | Two weekly reports had to be superseded after reading a half-filled last day | 2+ sites | active |
| FL-03 | all | Judge rankings per query, never by the site or page average: when a site starts showing for many new queries, its average worsens while impressions grow and every existing query holds. | A homepage average went 4.1 → 10.6 while its brand query improved 1.4 → 1.0 — new long-tail rows at 51–66 pulled the average; a "dropped" query was really two pages sharing it | reliable | active |
| FL-04 | all | On brand-heavy sites, click totals mostly measure brand demand. Split brand vs non-brand by query *and* by page: anonymised queries hide most name searches, so CTR curves built from query tables read several times too low. | One site: 88 % of clicks on hidden queries; an about page with 19 clicks and 0 visible queries in 3 months | 2+ sites | active |
| FL-05 | medical | Existing pages ranking 50+ didn't improve from on-page work alone. | Three treatment pages fell further after two rounds of on-page work. **Contrary:** later, two of them reached ~9–12 for two weeks after internal links and indexing requests only, no off-site work | once | disputed — confirm on 28 days |
| FL-06 | all | A section stuck in "Discovered – not indexed" while new articles index in a day is a crawl-capacity problem, not a links problem. Judge crawl tests in Crawl stats by Googlebot type — "Last crawl" in URL Inspection reflects the Smartphone crawler. | Six service pages linked site-wide stayed uncrawled for 6+ weeks; Desktop Googlebot fetched them after a footer change, Smartphone never did; ~70 % of Desktop fetches were prefetch payloads. Contrast: on a new shop, sitemap-only hubs stayed undiscovered while linked ones indexed | once | open |
| FL-07 | all | If a Search Console CSV export never arrives, the browser is probably asking where to save. Don't retry blind — read the Performance table directly (every row is in the page). | Two properties, two months apart: export clicked, no file after 30 s; tables read from the page instead | 2+ sites | active |
| FL-08 | all | WordPress SEO plugins build breadcrumb and article-section schema from the post's category — a wrong category mislabels the page's topic. Check it before working on a lead page. | 14 adult-treatment posts filed under a children's category; their breadcrumb schema said so | once | active |
| FL-09 | local, medical | Directories, booking platforms and map apps often already host profiles of the business that nobody manages — sometimes with the wrong category or specialty, no site link, and rivals promoted on them. They can outrank the site on its own name. The task is "claim and correct", not "create". A drafted claim does nothing until it is sent. | Four practices: unmanaged profiles found on 2–6 platforms each; one unclaimed profile took #1 on the doctor's full name while the claim sat drafted for a week; a map app already had two duplicate places | reliable | active |
| FL-10 | all | A name shared with other people or businesses caps the bare-name query at ~#3–5 (namesakes' map listings and knowledge panels sit above). Track and promise the name + city/specialty form. | Three sites: bare name #3–6 under namesakes; name + city #1–2 | reliable | active |
| FL-11 | all | One contextual link from a page that splits a query to the intended owner was followed by an ~8-position gain within a week — which reversed within three weeks. Try the link first, then decide on a merge with 28-day numbers. | Single query on a medical site; confounded by later edits | once | disputed |
| FL-12 | all | Scheduled jobs can disappear from a scheduler while their files stay on disk; nothing alerts, reports just stop. A missing report means "check the scheduler", not "late". | Five sites lost 2–3 jobs each over two weeks | reliable | active |
| FL-13 | all | Missed scheduled jobs fire together when the scheduler next runs, and a run marked "succeeded" can have written nothing. Never rely on slot order; judge a job by the file it wrote. | A plan job and its data job started within the same second; several "succeeded" runs of 7–84 s produced no file | reliable | active |
| FL-14 | medical | A public-domain clinical photo can be someone else's image under a wrong title, even when Wikipedia uses it. Reverse-search it before use, above all on a banner with no credit. | A "leukoplakia" photo matched a stock photo of a different, non-precancerous lesion; caught before deploy | once | active |
| FL-15 | all | Unattended runs can be refused a Search Console "Request indexing" click by the agent's own permission guard, even when the plan allows it. Plan indexing as a human item unless a permission rule allows it. | Two clicks refused on a scheduled run; the human did the 5 URLs by hand | once | active |
| FL-16 | all | Google blocks automated searches after ~7 queries a few minutes apart, and a batch with more searches queued keeps firing into the block. Max 8 per run, 60–120 s apart and varied, one per call, stop at the first block. | Block on the 8th search at a fixed ~25 s gap, twice on one site | once | active |
| FL-17 | all | A domain can drop out of DNS without warning: a registrar hold a day after the renewal date gave NXDOMAIN while the server kept serving by IP. Impressions held ~2.5 days, then fell 99 %. Check DNS and registration status every run. | Medical site, Nov 2026 | once | active |
| FL-18 | all | A storefront that maps every failed API read to "not found" serves 200 + `noindex` during deploys and outages. A noindex fetch drops an indexed page; a 5xx only delays it. | 13 product pages 200 + noindex during a 3-minute deploy | once | active |
| FL-19 | ecommerce | On a new shop, crawlable filter and sort links let Googlebot fetch thousands of noindexed combinations while sitemap products wait. `noindex, follow` stops indexing, not crawling: block the parameters in robots.txt. | 3,900 noindexed filter URLs vs 57 sitemap URLs "Discovered" on a 9-day-old domain | once | open |
| FL-20 | all | Next.js 15.2+ streams title, description and canonical into `<body>` for Googlebot on dynamically rendered pages, and URL Inspection's live test hides it. Check with a Googlebot UA; fix with `htmlLimitedBots`. Statically generated pages are unaffected. | One shop affected on every dynamic page type; a statically built site checked clean | once | active |
| FL-21 | all | A one-week jump of 10+ positions on a low-impression query, with no change to the page, often reverts the next week. Don't credit it or re-plan on it; judge on 28 days. | Two sites: 25 → 10.4 → 19.5; 76 → 45 → 72 | 2+ sites | active |
| FL-22 | medical | A new service × area page with a handover link from the old page that held the query, plus 2–3 internal links, reached the top 3 within ~a week on a query the site ranked 60+ for. It needs a site Google isn't demoting. | Two sites: new pages at 3.4–7.0 within a week. **Contrary:** a site under a core-update demotion got 0–12 impressions on three new pages | 2+ sites | open |
| FL-23 | all | A security plugin can return HTTP 500 to Google's crawlers only, while browsers, Bingbot and URL Inspection's live test get 200. Fetch `/` and `/robots.txt` with a Googlebot UA every run. Expect a fast but partial recovery after the fix. | Every Googlebot request 500 after a hosting move (old plugin vs patched PHP); fixed by a plugin update; impressions back within a day at −14 % | once | active |
| FL-24 | all | Finished work that never reaches the site or the platform was the biggest untapped lever across all seven sites, and a 2-week escalation didn't fix it. What came back fast was one short ask sent straight to the decider: ≤ 3 yes/no questions, each with the item's age. | 0 of 13 off-site items sent on three sites; direct asks to the owner answered in a day, "forward to the clinic" stalled 4 weeks | reliable | active |
| FL-25 | all | Next.js `<Link>` prefetch makes Googlebot fetch `?_rsc=` payloads that crowd out real pages on a small crawl budget. | 148 of 206 Desktop crawl rows in 90 days were `?_rsc=` requests | once | open |
| FL-26 | all | A changed page can stay uncrawled for weeks when its one indexing request hit the quota, so a traffic move after the change may be unrelated. Check last crawl before judging; start the 14/28-day clock at the first crawl after the change. | A rewritten page last crawled two months before its "verdict"; its drop started before the rewrite | once | active |
| FL-27 | medical | Ask what the practice legally is before choosing its descriptor (e.g. licensed clinic vs private practice) and keep it in one constant. The owner will reject the wrong one, and it spreads fast. | A wrong descriptor reached 5 titles, 14 metas, 168 body mentions and all schema in a month before the owner objected | reliable | active |
| FL-28 | medical | A page's impressions can belong to a different condition sharing its acronym or name. Read its own queries before choosing it for a rebuild; page impressions aren't demand. | A rare-condition page's 1,100 impressions were almost all for an unrelated procedure with the same acronym | once | active |
| FL-29 | all | When an agent drives a browser: connected-browser labels and order change mid-session, and two sessions driving the same extension switch it under each other. Select by device and OS explicitly before every batch, and confirm the signed-in account before reading a client's Search Console. | Labels moved between devices within an hour on several runs; one session's tabs landed in another's browser | reliable | active |
