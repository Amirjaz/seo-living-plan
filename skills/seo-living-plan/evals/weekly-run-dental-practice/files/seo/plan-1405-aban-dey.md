Profile: brand-heavy local practice in Punak; commercial queries sit mostly at 4–15 and 20+; the area head term «دندانپزشکی پونک» is the only commercial top-3. Directory profiles exist on Neshan and Paziresh24 but are unmanaged (L-009).

2. C. Local signals — evidence: Neshan place has the old address; Paziresh24 profile lists "general dentist" — targets: claim and correct both, add Balad
3. D. Medical trust — evidence: no reviewer box or council number on treatment pages
4. G. Snippet CTR — evidence: homepage CTR 15 % at position 2.6 vs site median 22 % at 1–3
Never: price figures on the site without the doctor's written OK; before/after photos without patient consent; «کلینیک» anywhere.
Targets (end of quarter): top 3 2–4 · top 10 4–6 · KPI 85–115 non-brand commercial clicks / 28 d (baseline 58 × 1.5–2.0, quarter of push-zone work)
