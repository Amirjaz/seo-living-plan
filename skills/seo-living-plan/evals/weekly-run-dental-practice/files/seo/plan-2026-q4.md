The practice moved in 2025: a duplicate Google Business Profile still shows the old address, and the Doctify profile is unmanaged (L-009).
1. A. Push zone — evidence: 5 commercial queries at 4–15 with ≥ 20 impr/28 d (baseline) — targets: dental implants leeds, veneers leeds, invisalign headingley
2. C. Local signals — evidence: duplicate Google listing at the old address; Doctify lists "orthodontist" — targets: remove the duplicate, correct Doctify, add Bing Places
3. D. Medical trust — evidence: no reviewer box or GDC number on treatment pages
4. G. Snippet CTR — evidence: homepage CTR 15 % at position 2.6 vs site median 22 % at 1–3
Never: price figures on the site without the dentist's written OK; before/after photos without patient consent; "clinic" or "cosmetic clinic" anywhere.
Targets (end of quarter): top 3 2–4 · top 10 4–6 · KPI 85–115 non-brand commercial clicks / 28 d (baseline 58 × 1.5–2.0, quarter of push-zone work)
